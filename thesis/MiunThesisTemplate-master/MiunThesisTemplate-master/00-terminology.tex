\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Working definition in this thesis} \\
\midrule
API & Application Programming Interface; a defined request/response boundary between software components. \\
bounded memory & Session-scoped, process-local access to the two latest completed interactions. \\
\texttt{CONFIG-BASELINE} & Inherited Yacoub-compatible minimal-agent comparator without the Obid runtime controls. \\
\texttt{CONFIG-OBID} & Obid-created tool-using decision configuration with bounded memory and executable release controls. \\
Decision Agent & The \texttt{CONFIG-OBID} AI Agent that proposes \texttt{emit\_action} or \texttt{no\_action}; it has no release authority. \\
deterministic policy & Fixed allowlist and approval-routing logic applied after successful validation. \\
HITL & Human-in-the-Loop; the runtime gate where an approval-required action remains held until an actual approve/deny decision. \\
IoT & Internet of Things; connected devices and services that sense context and may trigger actions \cite{perera2014context}. \\
JSON & JavaScript Object Notation, the text-based structured-data interchange format used by the contracts \cite{bray2017json}. \\
JSON Schema & A vocabulary for describing and validating structural constraints on JSON instances \cite{wright2022jsonschemavalidation}. \\
LLM & Large Language Model; the model class used by the workflow decision component. \\
middleware & The inherited service between workflow calls and the simulated action API. \\
n8n & The workflow-automation runtime used to connect triggers, decision nodes, controls, and actions \cite{n8nCreateRunWorkflows}. \\
\texttt{no\_action} & Internal decision meaning that no shared action is formed or released. \\
ReAct & A reasoning-and-acting pattern that interleaves model decisions, tool actions, and observations \cite{yao2023react}. \\
RQ & Research Question; one of the three questions operationalized by the evaluation. \\
runtime validation & Deterministic post-agent checks of a candidate against the frozen action constraints. \\
shared action interface & The frozen four-field action contract preserved across inherited and Obid components. \\
structured output & The machine-readable internal decision envelope returned by the Decision Agent. \\
